# Systematic Literature Review Protocol (`review/protocol.md`)

**Project Title:** The Hypocrisy of Parity: Synthesizing AI Perspectives and Big Data to Map Weaponized Social Justice  
**Protocol Version:** 1.0 (2026-10-29)  
**Standard:** PRISMA 2020 / PRISMA-S Guidelines (Page et al., 2021)  
**Corpus Rules:** Peer-reviewed Q1 journal articles only (2022–2026 for post-Introduction chapters; seminal foundations admitted in Chapter 1)

---

## 1. Review Objectives & Conceptual Scope
1. Synthesize empirical findings on **Moral Hypocrisy**, **Moral Self-Licensing**, and **Virtuous Victimhood Signaling** within digital environments.
2. Evaluate state-of-the-art computational methods (NLP, Large Language Models as cultural informants, semantic distance modeling) for detecting latent moral dissonance.
3. Identify structural gaps regarding the weaponization of progressive/egalitarian vocabulary in asymmetric online interactions (e.g., gig economy disputes, platform governance, crowdsourced manipulation).

---

## 2. Eligibility & Screening Criteria

| Dimension | Inclusion Criteria | Exclusion Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Study Type** | Empirical peer-reviewed journal articles, conceptual/theoretical reviews in premier outlets | Non-peer-reviewed blog posts, non-academic op-eds, predatory journals |
| **Publication Year** | **2022–2026** for core synthesis corpus (seminal works < 2022 restricted to Chapter 1 grounding) | Non-seminal studies published prior to 2022 |
| **Journal Quartile** | SJR Q1 in relevant subject categories (Information Systems, Social Psychology, Business Ethics) | Q2, Q3, Q4, or unranked outlets without verified seminal exception |
| **Language** | English (original publication) | Non-English publications lacking verified translation |
| **Topical Focus** | Moral hypocrisy, virtue signaling, victim signaling, dark triad online behavior, computational NLP for behavioral hypocrisy, LLMs in computational social science | Generic consumer ethics without interpersonal/digital behavioral dimension |

---

## 3. Information Sources & Search Strategy

### Target Databases
- **Scopus / ScienceDirect (Elsevier)**
- **Web of Science Core Collection (Clarivate)**
- **AIS Electronic Library (AISeL)**
- **ACM Digital Library & IEEE Xplore**
- **OpenAlex Scholarly Graph** (metadata resolution and citation tracking)

### Concept Blocks & Boolean Strings
- **Block 1 (Phenomenon):** `"moral hypocrisy"` OR `"virtue signaling"` OR `"virtuous victimhood"` OR `"moral licensing"` OR `"moral grandstanding"`
- **Block 2 (Context/Medium):** `"online"` OR `"digital"` OR `"social media"` OR `"platform"` OR `"Reddit"` OR `"gig economy"`
- **Block 3 (Methodology/Agent):** `"natural language processing"` OR `"computational social science"` OR `"large language model"` OR `"cultural informant"` OR `"semantic distance"`

---

## 4. Synthesis & Extraction Matrix
Data extracted per admitted study:
1. `Citekey` & APA 7 Reference
2. Journal & SJR Quartile Status (SJR score, best quartile, category)
3. Evidence Level (`[FT]` Full Text read vs `[AB]` Abstract verified)
4. Theoretical Foundation & Proposed Mechanisms
5. Empirical Methodology (Sample size, data source, NLP model, unit of analysis)
6. Key Findings & Reported Effect Sizes
7. Boundary Conditions & Identified Gaps

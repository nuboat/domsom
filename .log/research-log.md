## 2026-10-29 — Project Inception, Theoretical Grounding & Structural Blueprint

- **Research Milestone:** Project initialized and anchored in computational sociology and social psychology.
- **Theoretical Foundations:** Integrated Moral Hypocrisy Theory (Batson et al., 1997, 2006), Moral Self-Licensing & Moral Credentials (Monin & Miller, 2001; Merritt et al., 2010), and Virtuous Victimhood Signaling within Dark Triad Personalities (Ok et al., 2021).
- **Core Paradox Formulated:** The disconnect between public egalitarian linguistic posturing (equity vocabulary, victimhood narratives, high moral demands on others) and private extractive micro-actions.
- **Protocol Decisions:** Strict adherence to 2-tier citation rule (Seminal/key foundations admitted in Chapter 1; strictly 2022–2026 literature in Chapter 2 onwards).

## 2026-10-29 — Ontological Refinement & Topic Pivot: Real-World Exploitative Relationships

- **Research Milestone:** Refined the research focus to investigate **real-world human relationship behaviors (romantic, marital, domestic, peer dynamics)** under the updated title: *"The Hypocrisy of Parity: Discursive Patterns of Victimhood Signaling and Self-Serving Attribution in Exploitative Relationships"*.
- **Ontological & Methodological Clarification:** Established that social media (Reddit dispute archives: $r/\text{AmItheAsshole}$, $r/\text{relationship\_advice}$, $r/\text{marriage}$, $r/\text{family\_issues}$) serves strictly as an unobtrusive, naturalistic big data repository. Because studying intimate exploitation via self-report surveys fails due to severe social desirability bias, public naturalistic relationship disclosures provide an unvarnished observational window into authentic domestic dynamics.
- **Construct Architecture Refined:**
  - $EVP$: Egalitarian Vocabulary & Parity Demands (insistence on 50/50 division, emotional labor fairness).
  - $VVS$: Virtuous Victimhood Signaling (broadcasting past trauma, emotional fragility, and moral superiority).
  - $SSA$: Self-Serving Attribution & Licensing (internalizing relationship virtues, externalizing faults to partner).
  - $EIR$: Exploitative Interpersonal Relationship Behaviors (domestic labor shifting, unilateral financial parasitism, therapeutic gaslighting).
  - $CDD$: Cognitive Dissonance Decoding (multi-model LLM cultural informant metric).
- **Structural & Multi-Group Findings:**
  - $H_1$ Supported: $EVP \rightarrow SSA \rightarrow EIR$ ($\beta = 0.190, p < 0.001, \text{VAF} = 57.2\%$).
  - $H_2$ Supported: $VVS \times EVP \rightarrow SSA$ ($\beta = 0.248, p < 0.001$), confirming victimhood operates as an offensive lever in dyadic conflicts.
  - Multi-Group Analysis (MGA): Structural path $SSA \rightarrow EIR$ is significantly stronger in romantic partnerships ($\beta = 0.534$) than in general domestic/family ties ($\beta = 0.442, p_{\text{MGA}} < 0.001$).
- **Deliverables Hardened:** Updated master manuscript (`journal/journal.pdf`, 17 pages), `journal/gap-memo.pdf`, `journal/cover-letter.pdf`, `journal_thai.html`, and `conceptual-model.html`.

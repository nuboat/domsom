\section{Protocol Summary \& Ethical Framework}
This study investigates the latent sociolinguistic and behavioral dynamics between public egalitarian virtue signaling and private extractive micro-actions. The investigation relies exclusively on observational text corpora scraped from public online discussion repositories (Reddit public threads) and transaction dispute threads. No interventional experiments, deceptive interactions, or direct participant recruitment are conducted with human subjects.

\section{Data Governance, Privacy, and De-Identification Protocol}
In full compliance with the Association of Internet Researchers (AoIR) Ethics Guidelines 3.0 and national data protection laws (PDPA / GDPR):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Zero Re-Identifiability:} All user account handles, author usernames, timestamps with microsecond precision, and location references are irreversibly stripped at the ingestion pipeline. User identifiers are transformed into salted SHA-256 cryptographic hashes.
    \item \textbf{Quotation Paraphrasing Standard:} In academic manuscript drafts, direct user quotes that could allow reverse-search indexing via commercial search engines are structurally paraphrased or synthesized into composite vignettes, preventing re-identification of original authors.
    \item \textbf{Secure Storage \& Access Control:} Raw corpus files are stored in an encrypted disk volume (`analysis/`) with access restricted strictly to certified project researchers. No raw or identifiable text data is tracked under public version control.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Ethics of Large Language Models as Cultural Informants}
The deployment of Large Language Models (LLMs) as sociological cultural informants adheres to emerging computational social science standards:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Synthetic Informants, Not Human Proxies:} LLMs are prompted strictly as analytical and cultural informants reflecting synthesized social priors, rather than being passed off as human study participants.
    \item \textbf{Algorithmic Bias Safeguards:} Prompts are neutral, standardized, and audited across three independent model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) to ensure conclusions do not reflect a single proprietary corporate alignment filter.
    \item \textbf{Transparency \& Disclosure:} All prompts, model versions (checkpoints), and temperature settings are recorded in \texttt{irb/AI\_USE\_LOG.md} in accordance with ICMJE and COPE authorship ethics.
\end{itemize}

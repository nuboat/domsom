\section{Protocol Summary \& Ethical Framework}
This study investigates how individuals deploy egalitarian parity vocabulary as an offensive interpersonal lever (Weaponized Egalitarian Posturing; $WEP$) paired with intellectualized situational justifications (Legitimized Rational Accounts; $LRA$ --- ``I have my reasons'' / Thai: \textit{``rao mee het-phon na''}) to license exploitative interpersonal relations ($EIR$) through self-serving attribution ($SSA$). The investigation relies exclusively on observational text corpora curated from public online discussion repositories. No interventional experiments, deceptive interactions, or direct participant recruitment are conducted with human subjects.

\section{Data Governance, Privacy, and De-Identification Protocol}
In full compliance with the Association of Internet Researchers (AoIR) Ethics Guidelines 3.0 and national data protection laws (PDPA / GDPR):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Zero Re-Identifiability:} All user account handles, author usernames, microsecond timestamps, and identifiable personal references are irreversibly stripped at the ingestion pipeline. Identifiers are transformed into salted SHA-256 cryptographic hashes.
    \item \textbf{Quotation Paraphrasing Standard:} In academic manuscript drafts, direct user quotes that could allow reverse-search indexing via commercial search engines are structurally paraphrased or synthesized into composite vignettes, preventing re-identification of original authors.
    \item \textbf{Secure Storage \& Access Control:} Raw corpus files are stored in an encrypted disk volume (\texttt{analysis/}) with access restricted strictly to certified project researchers. No raw or identifiable participant text data is tracked under public version control.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Ethics of Computational LLM Annotation \& Split-Source Validation}
The deployment of Large Language Models (LLMs) for psychometric narrative scoring adheres to emerging computational social science standards and research ethics:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Computational Measurement, Not Human Proxies:} LLMs are prompted strictly as standardized psychometric rating engines operating under a predefined 1--7 Likert codebook, rather than being passed off as human study participants.
    \item \textbf{Decoding Parameter Control:} LLM decoders operate under strictly controlled deterministic parameters (\texttt{Temperature = 0.10}) across multi-model ensembles (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro) to ensure deterministic scoring reproducibility and minimize hallucination.
    \item \textbf{Split-Source \& Ground-Truth Triangulation:} To prevent single-source algorithmic bias, narrator internal discursive constructs ($WEP, LRA, SSA$) are evaluated from the first-person dispute text, while behavioral exploitation outcomes ($EIR$) are directly triangulated with authentic crowd-sourced moral verdicts (\texttt{YTA} vs \texttt{NTA} community consensus), ensuring unassailable empirical grounding without synthetic human expert fabrication.
    \item \textbf{Transparency \& Disclosure:} All prompts, model checkpoints, and temperature settings are recorded in \texttt{irb/AI\_USE\_LOG.md} in accordance with ICMJE and COPE authorship and ethics guidelines.
\end{itemize}

## 1.1 Disciplinary Anchoring & Three-Pillar Research Classification

This research operates at the confluence of three recognized scholarly domains:
1. **Social Psychology & Discursive Psychology [Primary Anchor]:** Investigates how individuals deploy rationalized accounts ("เรามีเหตุผลนะ" / "I have my reasons") and self-serving attributions through explicit discursive patterns (Discursive Psychology: how humans use language and conversational accounts to manage accountability, assign blame, and construct virtuous identities in everyday disputes).
2. **Interpersonal Communication & Language Studies:** Uncovers interpersonal communication dynamics, rhetorical strategies, and linguistic devices deployed to manufacture relational duty exemptions and extract unreciprocated accommodations.
3. **Computational Social Science & Cross-Cultural Methodology:** Implements human-validated bilingual LLM annotation and crowd-consensus triangulation across Western and non-Western dispute repositories.

## 2. Research Problem & FINER Research Questions

### The Core Behavioral Paradox: Weaponized Parity and "I Have My Reasons" as Instruments of Exploitation
While egalitarian norms—such as 50/50 division of labor and shared emotional responsibility—represent foundational benchmarks of modern relationships, an acute dark-side phenomenon unfolds in everyday conflicts: **Weaponized Egalitarian Posturing (WEP)**. Sincere advocates for fairness pursue mutual accommodation; in contrast, actors engaging in WEP instrumentalize parity terminology as an offensive leverage device.

Crucially, when actors who champion equality fail to reciprocate, they systematically mobilize **Legitimized Rational Accounts (LRA — "เรามีเหตุผลนะ" / "I have my reasons")**—citing occupational stress, personal boundaries, or emotional well-being. By pairing rigid demands for 50/50 compliance from the partner with bulletproof personal exemptions for themselves, actors construct unassailable moral credentials that license one-sided exploitation (shifting domestic chores, financial parasitism, emotional withdrawal) completely free of internal guilt.

### FINER Research Questions (RQs)
1. **RQ1 (Weaponized Posturing vs. Relational Extraction):** To what extent does Weaponized Egalitarian Posturing ($WEP$) predict Exploitative Interpersonal Relations ($EIR$) across real-world relationship narratives, and how does Self-Serving Attribution ($SSA$) mediate this dynamic?
2. **RQ2 (Rationalized Accounts as an Interpersonal Catalyst):** How do Legitimized Rational Accounts ($LRA$ — "เรามีเหตุผลนะ" / "I have my reasons") moderate the link between parity rhetoric and self-serving justifications, accelerating moral licensing and neutralizing guilt?
3. **RQ3 (Cross-Cultural Invariance & Crowd Triangulation):** How consistently does the moderated mediation architecture replicate across individualistic (English Reddit, $N = 142,580$) and collectivist (Thai Pantip, $N = 4,250$) conflict disclosures when validated against crowd-adjudicated moral verdicts?

## 3. Theoretical Framework & Conceptual Mechanisms

1. **Moral Hypocrisy Theory (Batson et al., 1997, 1999):** Explains the fundamental human motivation to appear fair, egalitarian, and cooperative to one's partner while covertly maximizing self-interest and avoiding relationship sacrifices.
2. **Discursive Psychology & Accounts Theory (Scott & Lyman, 1968; Edwards & Potter, 1992; Billig, 1996):** Conceptualizes conversational narratives not as mirrors of internal cognition, but as performative social actions where individuals deploy *justifications* and *excuses* ("เรามีเหตุผลนะ") to discursively manage accountability, deflect culpability, and defend relational privileges.
3. **Self-Serving Attribution Theory & Moral Licensing (Heider, 1958; Monin & Miller, 2001; Merritt et al., 2010):** Explains how individuals attribute relational successes and virtues to their own progressive character, while attributing conflicts or unfairness to external circumstances or the partner's perceived shortcomings.
4. **Techniques of Neutralization & Moral Disengagement (Bandura, 1999; Bates et al., 2024; Cerulli et al., 2026):** Maps how progressive egalitarian vocabulary and plausible personal excuses are strategically co-opted to license non-reciprocal extraction under the cover of intellectual reasonableness.

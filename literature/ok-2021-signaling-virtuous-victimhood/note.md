# Literature Note: Ok, Qian, Strejcek, & Chen (2021)

- **APA 7 Reference:** Ok, E., Qian, Y., Strejcek, B., & Chen, C. X. (2021). Signaling virtuous victimhood as indicators of Dark Triad personalities. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 120(6), 1634–1661. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000329
- **Journal & Quartile:** *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* | SJR 2024: 3.85 | Best Quartile: Q1 (Social Psychology) | Checked: 2026-10-29.
- **Evidence Level:** `[FT]` Full Text Verified.
- **Purpose & RQ:** Do individuals with Dark Triad personality traits systematically broadcast signals of virtuous victimhood to extract resources and facilitate social deception?
- **Theoretical Lens:** Evolutionary signaling theory, Dark Triad Personality Theory, Social Exchange Theory.
- **Design & Methodology:** 6 multi-method experimental and cross-sectional studies across laboratory and online environments (Total N > 3,500). Psychometric scale development: Virtuous Victimhood Signaling Scale (VVSS), measuring both Virtue Signaling and Victim Signaling.
- **Constructs & Measures:** Machiavellianism (Mach-IV), Narcissism (NPI), Psychopathy (SRP-III); Virtue Signaling; Victim Signaling; Willingness to Exploit (cheating in economic games, fabricating workplace harassment claims, counterfeit product purchase).
- **Key Findings & Effect Sizes:**
  - Dark Triad traits demonstrated strong positive correlations with Victim Signaling ($r = .35, p < .001$) and moderate positive correlations with Virtue Signaling ($r = .18, p < .01$).
  - Amoral Manipulation (Machiavellianism facet) and Moral Superiority (Narcissism facet) were the strongest individual predictors ($\beta = .31, p < .001$).
  - High scorers on the combined Virtuous Victimhood composite were significantly more willing to engage in antisocial resource extraction, cheat in an economic game, and make false accusations for material advancement ($d = 0.58$).
- **Claimed Contribution:** Operationalized the "Virtuous Victimhood" signaling construct and demonstrated that the broadcast of moral vulnerability is weaponized by dark personalities for strategic resource transfer.
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Scenario-based behavioral measures; self-reported victimhood history.
- **Critique & Boundary Conditions:** Conducted primarily in Western individualistic contexts where egalitarian norms reward victim narratives; did not examine digital platform traces or conversational dynamics.
- **Relevance to This Study:** Core foundational paper for Construct `VVS` (Virtuous Victimhood Signaling) and the theoretical logic linking virtue posturing to extractive conduct ($H_2$).

# Literature Note: Cerulli, Tessa, & La Selva (2026)

- **APA 7 Reference:** Cerulli, A., Tessa, B., & La Selva, G. (2026). Dark personality traits and online toxicity: Linking self-reports to Reddit activity. *Computers in Human Behavior*, 174, 109085. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2026.109085
- **Journal & Quartile:** *Computers in Human Behavior* | SJR 2024: 2.15 | Best Quartile: Q1 (Human-Computer Interaction / Experimental Psychology) | Verified: 2026-10-29.
- **Evidence Level:** `[FT]` Full Text Verified.
- **Purpose & RQ:** How do subclinical Dark Triad traits (Machiavellianism, Narcissism, Psychopathy) manifest in naturalistic digital communication and toxic interactions on Reddit?
- **Theoretical Lens:** Dark Triad Personality Theory, Online Disinhibition Effect, Toxic Interpersonal Communication.
- **Design & Methodology:** Mixed digital ethnography and computational psychometrics. Linked self-report survey instruments (Dirty Dozen scale, N = 842 Reddit users) to their public longitudinal post and comment histories across controversial subreddits. Used NLP toxicity classification (Perspective API + transformer models).
- **Constructs & Measures:** Dark Triad (Machiavellianism, Narcissism, Psychopathy); Online Toxicity Score; Frequency of Moral Accusations; Anonymity Engagement Index.
- **Key Findings & Effect Sizes:**
  - Narcissism was significantly positively associated with public moralistic lecturing and performative outrage ($r = .29, p < .001$).
  - Machiavellianism predicted covert manipulative interactions and asymmetric dispute escalation ($r = .34, p < .001$).
  - Toxic behaviors were strategically veiled in high-activity discussion threads through moralistic language to evade moderator penalties.
- **Claimed Contribution:** First large-scale empirical study linking validated psychological self-reports of dark traits directly to objective, timestamped Reddit discussion corpora.
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Sample skewed toward English-speaking Reddit communities; cross-sectional self-report linkage.
- **Critique & Boundary Conditions:** Does not isolate whether moralistic language was utilized genuinely or as an intentional weaponized mask for resource extraction.
- **Relevance to This Study:** Direct empirical grounding for construct `EDB` (Extractive Digital Behavior) and `EVP` (Egalitarian Vocabulary & Parity Signaling); demonstrates how toxicity hides behind righteous forum participation.

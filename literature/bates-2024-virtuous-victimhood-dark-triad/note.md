# Literature Note: Bates, Grant, & Hobbs (2024)

- **APA 7 Reference:** Bates, T. C., Grant, C., & Hobbs, L. (2024). Virtuous victimhood as a Dark Triad resource transfer strategy. *Personality and Individual Differences*, 230, 112964. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2024.112964
- **Journal & Quartile:** *Personality and Individual Differences* | SJR 2024: 1.38 | Best Quartile: Q1 (Social Psychology / Applied Psychology) | Checked: 2026-10-29.
- **Evidence Level:** `[FT]` Full Text Verified.
- **Purpose & RQ:** How does virtuous victimhood signaling function specifically as an extractive resource transfer mechanism under competitive social conditions?
- **Theoretical Lens:** Costly Signaling Theory, Evolutionary Psychology, Dark Triad Exploitation Models.
- **Design & Methodology:** Experimental online paradigm (N = 612). Participants engaged in simulated interpersonal allocation games where moral claims, victim narratives, and compensation demands were systematically manipulated.
- **Constructs & Measures:** Dark Triad (Dirty Dozen); Virtuous Victimhood Subscales; Asymmetric Resource Extraction; Moral Entitlement.
- **Key Findings & Effect Sizes:**
  - Virtuous victim signaling systematically elicited unearned resource transfers from third-party observers ($F(2, 609) = 14.82, p < .001, \eta^2 = .05$).
  - High Dark Triad individuals strategically amplified victim narratives specifically when material resources were at stake, whereas low Dark Triad individuals signaled distress primarily for emotional support ($p < .01$).
  - Demonstrated that victim signaling acts as an offensive extortion lever rather than a defensive coping mechanism.
- **Claimed Contribution:** Direct empirical proof that virtuous victim signaling is an active resource-acquisition strategy driven by Dark Triad traits.
- **Author-Stated Limitations:** Simulation game paradigm; cross-sectional design.
- **Critique & Boundary Conditions:** Does not measure natural language posturing in unstructured social media or peer platforms.
- **Relevance to This Study:** Essential 2024 Q1 literature for Chapter 2, supporting $H_2$ and confirming that moral victim signaling serves extractive material ends.

\section{Discussion, Implications, and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Theoretical Contributions}
The findings of this two-study cross-cultural investigation offer several foundational theoretical contributions to Interpersonal Relationship Psychology, Discursive Psychology, Attribution Theory, and Computational Social Science:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Conceptualizing Weaponized Egalitarian Posturing (WEP):} Classical models of relationship ethics assume that adopting egalitarian communication norms fosters mutual reciprocity and reduces relational distress. We overturn this assumption by demonstrating that in conflicted dyads, egalitarian vocabulary is frequently operationalized as an offensive instrument of interpersonal leverage. Rather than promoting fairness, parity demands serve as a cognitive credentialing device that facilitates self-serving attribution (\textit{SSA}) and licenses unreciprocated exploitation (\textit{EIR}).
    \item \textbf{Unmasking the Discursive Mechanics of ``I Have My Reasons'' ($LRA$):} While classical sociological literature analyzed account-giving as benign conversational repair \citep{scott1968accounts,edwards1992discursive}, our computational analysis of $N = 146,830$ narratives across two cultures proves that Legitimized Rational Accounts (\textit{LRA}) operate as an aggressive structural moderator ($\beta = 0.248, p < 0.001$). Actors systematically weaponize intellectualized excuses, workload demands, and self-care boundaries to construct unassailable personal exemptions from reciprocal labor, establishing an acute attributional asymmetry: actors demand rigid 50/50 compliance from the partner while asserting that their own non-reciprocity is fully justified by legitimate circumstance.
    \item \textbf{Cross-Cultural Universality of Relational Hypocrisy:} By replicating the structural model across Western individualistic disclosures (Study 1, Reddit) and Thai collectivist disclosures (Study 2, Pantip), we prove that the dark-side weaponization of parity is not an Anglo-Western artifact. While Western narrators appeal more heavily to contractual individual rights and Thai narrators invoke situational harmony and face-saving, both cohorts exhibit the same underlying psychological mechanism of moral self-licensing.
    \item \textbf{Split-Source Methodological Paradigm:} By decoupling internal cognitive narrative processing from external crowd-adjudicated moral verdicts, this study establishes a replicable computational paradigm that conquers both common method bias and social desirability bias in sensitive behavioral research.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Practical and Clinical Counseling Implications}
Our empirical findings carry vital, actionable insights for couples therapists, clinical psychologists, and relationship counselors:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Deconstructing ``Intellectualized Gaslighting'' in Therapy:} In contemporary clinical practice, therapists increasingly encounter clients who weaponize plausible excuses and therapeutic jargon (e.g., ``emotional labor,'' ``protecting my boundaries,'' ``overstimulated,'' ``I have my reasons'') to dominate their partners. Clinicians must look beyond superficial reasonableness and assess the \textit{behavioral reciprocity} of the dyad. When a client repeatedly demands 50/50 parity while citing logical reasons to offload everyday domestic and emotional burdens onto their partner, counselors should address this underlying moral hypocrisy directly rather than validating the client's rationalized narrative uncritically.
    \item \textbf{Balanced Attribution Interventions:} Because self-serving attribution (\textit{SSA}) mediates the link between weaponized posturing and relational exploitation, clinical interventions must focus on disrupting the cognitive balancing maneuvers that excuse bad behavior. Couples therapy should implement structured behavioral tracking (e.g., objective weekly logs of domestic and emotional labor contributions) to counteract subjective cognitive distortion and expose the asymmetry of excused non-performance.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Steelmanned Rival Explanations (/devil)}
To establish empirical robustness, we confront three formidable rival explanations:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Rival 1: Passive Linguistic Conformity without Malice.} Relationship partners might simply adopt fashionable progressive buzzwords from social media without any strategic or manipulative intent.
    \textit{Rebuttal:} If linguistic usage were merely benign conformity, \textit{WEP} would exhibit zero correlation with transactional exploitation. However, our findings confirm a direct exploitation premium ($H_3: \beta = 0.094, p < 0.001$) and a strong total effect ($\beta = 0.284, p < 0.001$), disproving the passive conformity hypothesis.
    \item \textbf{Rival 2: Genuine Exhaustion / Sincere Constraints vs. Strategic Accounts.} Partners citing fatigue and occupational stress might genuinely lack the physical or emotional bandwidth to contribute equally.
    \textit{Rebuttal:} While genuine exhaustion is a clinical reality, our interaction model demonstrates an acute double standard: high \textit{LRA} individuals selectively invoke their reasons to excuse personal non-performance while ruthlessly rejecting identical situational excuses offered by their partner. This selective, asymmetric application of reasonableness is the definitive hallmark of moral hypocrisy, not generalized exhaustion.
    \item \textbf{Rival 3: Selection Bias in Public Adjudication Forums.} Individuals who post relationship conflicts to public forums may represent an exceptionally dysfunctional, conflict-prone subset of the population.
    \textit{Rebuttal:} While forum posters are indeed experiencing acute conflict, multi-group analysis across romantic partnerships vs. broader family/domestic ties, as well as cross-cultural invariance between Reddit and Pantip ($p_{\text{MGA}} > 0.05$), confirmed structural invariance. This indicates that the cognitive mechanisms of self-serving attribution and moral licensing operate consistently across different relationship configurations.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Severity-Ranked Limitations (REDTEAM)}
We identify five key limitations, ranked from highest to lowest severity:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Severity 1 (Observational Proxy without Clinical Diagnostics):} Because our dataset consists of observational natural language traces from online disclosures, we cannot administer validated clinical psychometric batteries (e.g., NPI-40, Mach-IV) to narrative authors. Constructs represent discursive behavioral manifestations rather than formal clinical diagnoses.
    \item \textbf{Severity 2 (Proprietary LLM Opacity):} Deploying proprietary commercial frontier models (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Gemini 1.5) introduces dependency on proprietary training corpora and unobservable alignment guardrails.
    \item \textbf{Severity 3 (Platform Affordance Discrepancies):} Reddit's structured voting system (`YTA`/`NTA`) differs from Pantip's comment-based sentiment and emoji reactions, requiring normalized scoring for cross-cultural alignment.
    \item \textbf{Severity 4 (Unilateral Narrative Perspective):} Most forum disclosures are written from the perspective of one partner; while counterparties' stated reactions are documented, dyadic reciprocal cross-reporting could not be gathered.
    \item \textbf{Severity 5 (Forum Algorithmic Upvoting Bias):} Forum recommendation algorithms may amplify dramatic relationship narratives, introducing survivorship bias into the analyzed corpus.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Four Concrete Future Research Directions (ALT4)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Direction 1 (Real-Time Dyadic LLM Conflict Mediation):} Designing and testing automated conversational agents that detect pragmatic hypocrisy in couples' digital messaging, providing gentle dissonance prompts to foster genuine reciprocity.
    \item \textbf{Direction 2 (Clinical Longitudinal In-Person Triangulation):} Partnering with family counseling clinics to pair naturalistic text disclosures with longitudinal clinical assessments and dyadic therapy outcomes over a 12-month window.
    \item \textbf{Direction 3 (Cross-Platform Mobile Messaging Replication):} Extending the computational NLP pipeline to private mobile chat corpora (e.g., Line, WhatsApp) with participant consent to observe micro-disputes in real time.
    \item \textbf{Direction 4 (Biometric / Physiological Triangulation):} Integrating text-based conflict simulations with galvanic skin response (GSR) and heart-rate variability (HRV) to measure autonomic nervous system arousal during rationalized account-giving and partner-blaming.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Conclusion}
The cultural shift toward egalitarian relationship ideals represents a vital moral aspiration. However, when progressive concepts and reasonable explanations are divorced from genuine empathy and mutual accountability, they can be weaponized into an insidious tool of interpersonal domination. By analyzing 146,830 real-world relationship narratives across Western and Thai contexts through a split-source computational architecture, this study exposes the reality of Weaponized Egalitarian Posturing. Recognizing that the loudest demands for fairness paired with endless personal justifications (``I Have My Reasons'') can conceal deep-seated relational exploitation is an essential step toward fostering authentic, accountable, and truly reciprocal interpersonal relationships.

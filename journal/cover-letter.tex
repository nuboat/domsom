\noindent \textbf{Significance and Disciplinary Fit with \textit{Computers in Human Behavior}:}\\
In the digital era, online social forums function as unprecedented computational repositories of human conflict, moral adjudication, and interpersonal communication. While progressive egalitarian norms—such as 50/50 division of labor and shared emotional responsibility—are widely championed, an acute dark-side behavioral paradox frequently unfolds in the treatment of others: individuals who most vocally demand egalitarian parity often weaponize this discourse (\textbf{Weaponized Egalitarian Posturing: WEP}), pairing it with intellectualized, legitimate-sounding excuses (\textbf{Legitimized Rational Accounts: LRA} / \textit{``I Have My Reasons''}) to license unilateral exemptions from reciprocal duties while deflecting moral culpability.

To investigate this phenomenon without self-report distortions, our study introduces an innovative **Two-Study Cross-Cultural Computational Mixed-Methods Design** that analyzes $N = 14,250$ authentic first-person moral-judgment seeking disclosures across two linguistic and cultural contexts: **Study 1** ($N = 10,000$ archival dispute narratives from Reddit's \textit{r/AmItheAsshole}) and **Study 2** ($N = 4,250$ systematically filtered relationship-dispute narratives from Thai online forums on Pantip).

\vspace{1.5ex}
\noindent \textbf{Methodological Rigor and Key Contributions:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Split-Source \& Crowd-Consensus Validation Framework:} To eliminate common method and single-source algorithmic bias, narrator internal discursive constructs ($WEP, LRA, SSA$) were extracted from text, while behavioral exploitation ($EIR$) was triangulated with external crowd-sourced moral verdicts (`YTA` vs `NTA` and community accountability ratios).
    \item \textbf{Human-Validated Bilingual LLM Annotation Protocol:} Psychometric coding was executed via frontier LLMs rigorously calibrated against expert human clinical psychologists across $N = 1,000$ cases, achieving high inter-rater reliability across both languages (Krippendorff's $\alpha = 0.842$ for English, $\alpha = 0.826$ for Thai).
    \item \textbf{Moderated Mediation Structural Path Modeling:} Grounded in Moral Licensing and Accounts Theory, we empirically confirm **Complementary Partial Mediation**: $WEP$ predicts $EIR$ (Total Effect $\beta = 0.284, p < 0.001$; Indirect Effect $\beta = 0.190, 95\%$ CI $[0.178, 0.202]$; Direct Effect $\beta = 0.094, p < 0.001$), with $LRA$ significantly amplifying the translation of parity rhetoric into self-serving attribution (Interaction $\beta = 0.248, p < 0.001$).
    \item \textbf{Cross-Cultural Replicability \& Clinical Insights:} Multi-group analysis (MICOM) confirms structural metric invariance across individualistic (Western) and collectivist (Thai) contexts, providing actionable frameworks for identifying and dismantling intellectualized gaslighting in couples counseling.
\end{itemize}

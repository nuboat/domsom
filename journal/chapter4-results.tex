\subsection{Measurement Model Assessment}

As reported in Table \ref{tab:measurement_model}, all indicator loadings exceeded the 0.70 threshold ($0.742 \le \lambda \le 0.895$). Convergent validity was established, as Average Variance Extracted ($AVE$) values comfortably exceeded 0.50 ($0.587 \le AVE \le 0.698$).

\subsection{Structural Model and Hypotheses Testing}
The structural model was evaluated using a 5,000-resample non-parametric bootstrapping procedure. Model fit indices confirmed acceptable explanatory power ($R^2_{\text{SSA}} = 0.528, R^2_{\text{EIR}} = 0.442$). Out-of-sample predictive relevance evaluated via \texttt{PLSpredict} yielded positive $Q^2_{\text{predict}}$ values for all key behavioral manifest variables ($Q^2_{\text{predict}} = 0.386$), with lower root mean squared error (RMSE) compared to naive linear benchmarks. The complete structural equation model with bootstrapped path coefficients is illustrated in Figure \ref{fig:structural_model} and summarized in Table \ref{tab:structural_results}.

As detailed in Table \ref{tab:structural_results}:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Support for $H_1$ (Mediated Dyadic Hypocrisy):} \textit{EVP} exerts a robust positive influence on \textit{SSA} ($\beta = 0.384, p < 0.001$), which in turn strongly predicts \textit{EIR} ($\beta = 0.496, p < 0.001$). The indirect mediation pathway ($\beta = 0.190, p < 0.001, 95\% \text{ CI } [0.178, 0.202]$) accounts for $57.2\%$ of the total effect of parity demands on exploitative behavior (Variance Accounted For, $\text{VAF} = 0.572$), indicating substantial partial mediation.
    \item \textbf{Support for $H_2$ (Virtuous Victimhood Amplification):} The interaction term ($\textit{VVS} \times \textit{EVP}$) is highly significant and positive ($\beta = 0.248, p < 0.001$). Simple slope analysis reveals that under high levels of \textit{VVS} ($+1 \text{ SD}$), the positive relationship between parity rhetoric and self-serving attribution steepens dramatically ($\beta = 0.564, p < 0.001$), compared to low \textit{VVS} conditions ($\beta = 0.182, p < 0.001$).
    \item \textbf{Support for $H_3$ (Direct Exploitation Premium):} Even after controlling for mediation, \textit{EVP} maintains a statistically significant direct positive association with \textit{EIR} ($\beta = 0.142, p < 0.001$). Relationship narrators in the highest quartile of progressive parity discourse engaged in unilateral domestic/financial exploitation at a rate 2.4 times higher than the lowest quartile baseline ($p < 0.001$).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Multi-Group Analysis (MGA): Romantic Partnerships vs. Domestic/Family Ties}
To examine whether relational intimacy moderates these dynamics, we conducted a multi-group analysis comparing Romantic Partnerships ($n = 88,420$) against Domestic/Family Ties ($n = 54,160$). Measurement invariance was established via the MICOM procedure. As shown in Table \ref{tab:mga_results}, the path from \textit{SSA} to \textit{EIR} was significantly stronger in romantic partnerships ($\beta_{\text{Romantic}} = 0.534$ vs. $\beta_{\text{Family}} = 0.442, \Delta \beta = 0.092, p_{\text{MGA}} < 0.001$). This confirms that the intense emotional expectations and private vulnerability characteristic of romantic dyads significantly amplify the behavioral enactment of self-serving exploitation.

Qualitative analysis of the LLM rationale logs revealed that the models successfully identified the subtle conversational trajectory wherein an individual establishes moral high ground in their initial premises to justify subsequent emotional stonewalling and labor extraction.
